AI models

Edit any row and click Save settings. Pick the model for each job from one place: General Queries (the assistant and anything without a phase model), the pipeline's Inclusion phase, and its Exclusion phase. A phase with no model set falls back to the General Queries model. All prices are USD per 1M tokens; the standard rate used for cost estimates is input cache-miss off-peak and output off-peak.
