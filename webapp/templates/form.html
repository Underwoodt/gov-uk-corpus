Organisation slugs, comma-separated or one per line (e.g. environment-agency). Leave blank to search all organisations.

How this narrows your search

A page is kept if one of these organisations published it or is named on it. Everything after this point looks only at those pages, so a tighter choice means a faster, cheaper run and fewer irrelevant results. Use the exact slug (the last part of the org's gov.uk path).

Also search the pages of any department that sits under an organisation above (e.g. ticking this with department-for-environment-food-rural-affairs also searches Environment Agency, Natural England, Rural Payments Agency, and the rest).

How this narrows your search

One term per line. A term is a single word, or a pair of words that sit next to each other.

How your terms are matched

This is a word match, not a search for a phrase or an idea. We look through the title and text of every page for the terms you list. A page with none of them is skipped.

Each line is one word (slurry) or two words that appear together (catch certificate). Three or more words never match. Word endings are matched, so lagoon also finds lagoons. Capital letters do not matter.

  • Avoid broad words such as farm, water, waste. They match thousands of pages.
  • Prefer the specific term: slurry, lagoon, catch certificate.

Describe your topic in your own words. Be generous: this is about keeping anything relevant.

How to write this well

Say what the thing is. Do not insist the page is mainly about your topic — a single mention can still be the page you need. Do not list what you are not interested in; that belongs in "What to exclude".

Slurry is liquid or semi-liquid livestock manure: cattle and pig slurry, dirty water, digestate from farm anaerobic digestion, and silage effluent. Include a page if it mentions slurry in this farming sense anywhere, even once.

List the things that look relevant but are not. Be as specific as you can.

How to write this well

Words with another meaning (coal slurry), the wrong subject area (sewage sludge when you mean animal slurry), or pages that mention your topic once in passing. Being concrete works better.

Exclude pages where slurry means something other than livestock manure: coal, mining, concrete slurry. Exclude sewage sludge and biosolids.
