GDS Compliance (Non-LLM)

Plain-English quality and freshness of the pages at a chosen level of the funnel. Quality is a 1–5 star rating (5 = healthy) from the plain-English issue classes below, weighted by severity and normalised by page length. Freshness is by last public update.

What these checks mean & how quality is scored

Each page's body text is scanned for classes of plain-English problem. Every occurrence is counted per class, turned into an impact of weight × log₂(1 + count) (so a few serious issues outweigh many trivial ones), and the total impact per 1,000 words maps to a 1–5 star rating. Weights: 3 = high impact, 1 = low.
