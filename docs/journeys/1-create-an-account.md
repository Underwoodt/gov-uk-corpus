# Journey 1: Create an account

*What this shows: how a new person gets into the Shortlist Builder for the first time.*

**Who / when:** anyone with a DEFRA or Equal Experts email who has been pointed at the tool.
**Pages used:** Sign in (`/login`), Register (`/register`), Forgot password (`/forgot-password`).

# Journey Overview

Getting in takes about a minute. You register once with your DEFRA or Equal Experts email, which
keeps every account tied to a real colleague, and you land on the Shortlists page ready to work. If
you ever forget your password, there's a reset link on the sign-in page.

---

## 1.1: Land on Sign in

> "Everything sits behind a sign-in, so nothing here is public. If you already have an account
> you'd log in here; the first time, you register."

**What you do:** open the Shortlist Builder link. If you're not signed in, it takes you straight to
the **Sign in** page.

**What you see:** an email and password box, a **Forgot password** link, and, for newcomers, a link
to **create an account**.

![Screenshot: the Sign in page, with email and password boxes, a "Forgot password" link, and a "create an account" link](screenshots/1-1-sign-in.png)

## 1.2: Register with your work email

> "It only accepts a DEFRA or Equal Experts address, so accounts always map to real colleagues.
> There's a suggested strong password too; copy it into a password manager and you're done."

**What you do:** follow **create an account** to the **Register** page and fill in four things: your
**First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, and a **Password** (with **Confirm password**).

**What you see:** a note that you must use your DEFRA or Equal Experts email address, and a suggested
password you can copy if you'd rather not think one up.

![Screenshot: the Register form, with First name, Last name, Email, Password and Confirm, the "use your DEFRA or Equal Experts email" note, and the suggested password](screenshots/1-2-register.png)

**Note:** the email restriction is the tool's front-door guest list. An address outside those two
domains is turned away here rather than later.

## 1.3: Submit and you're in

> "That's us in, looking at the Shortlists page. From here everything is about building and
> reading shortlists."

**What you do:** submit the form. Your account is created and you're signed in.

**What you see:** the **Shortlists** home page, listing the shortlists (empty if you're the first in,
or your team's existing ones if not). That's the start of [Journey 2: What is a shortlist?](2-what-is-a-shortlist.md).

![Screenshot: the Shortlists home page just after signing in, with the "Build a new shortlist" button](screenshots/1-3-shortlists-home.png)

## 1.4: If you get stuck signing in

> "Forgot your password? The usual reset-by-email link is right there on the sign-in page, so
> you don't need to ask anyone."

**What you do:** use **Forgot password** on the Sign in page to get a reset link by email.

![Screenshot: the Forgot password page, where you enter your email to get a reset link](screenshots/1-4-forgot-password.png)
